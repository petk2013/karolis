Frequently asked questions

How quickly will we see leads?

Campaigns launch within 14 days of kickoff. Most clients see their first qualified conversations in week 3, with consistent volume by week 6–8.

What counts as a “qualified” lead?

We define qualification criteria with you upfront — company size, budget, decision-maker role, and intent. Only leads matching those criteria reach your calendar.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. We work month-to-month after an initial 90-day ramp period. We keep clients with results, not contracts.

What industries do you work with?

We work with B2B services, SaaS, professional services and local businesses with a clear offer and a sales process ready to handle more volume.

How do you charge?

A flat monthly retainer plus a performance component tied to qualified leads delivered. You'll get exact pricing in your free growth plan — no surprises.
