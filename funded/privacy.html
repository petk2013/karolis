Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

Ascendra Futures (“we”, “us”) provides funded trading evaluation programs. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and how we use it. Contact us at support@ascendra.example.

2. Data we collect

When you start an evaluation we collect your name, email address and the account size you selected. If you become a funded trader we additionally collect identity verification documents and payout details, as required to pay you and to meet our compliance obligations.

3. How we use your data

We use your information to create and administer your trading account, process payouts, provide support, and send service updates about your account. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Trading data

We record the trading activity on your evaluation and funded accounts. This is used to enforce program rules, calculate payouts, and improve the program. Aggregated, anonymised statistics may be published; individual trading data is not shared publicly without your consent.

5. Retention

Account and trading records are kept for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only for the period required by applicable financial record-keeping rules.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing support@ascendra.example. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Risk disclosure. Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss. Ascendra Futures is a fictional company used as a website template; all figures shown are illustrative placeholders.
