The rules

Four rules. That's the whole rulebook.

No hidden clauses designed to fail you. Break none of these and the account stays yours.

Trailing drawdown

Your maximum loss level trails your highest closed balance until it reaches your starting balance, then it locks. It never trails past break-even.

Daily loss limit

A soft stop that ends your trading day if hit. It resets at the next session open — hitting it does not fail your account.

Five trading days minimum

You need at least five days with a filled trade before passing. This stops a single lucky session from qualifying an account.

Flat before the close

Close all positions before the daily settlement window. No overnight or weekend exposure on funded accounts.

Payouts

FAQ

Questions traders ask

Is my own money at risk?

Only the monthly evaluation fee. Trading losses on both the evaluation and the funded account are ours, not yours — you can never owe us money from a losing trade.

What happens if I break a rule?

Hitting the daily loss limit simply ends your trading day. Breaching the maximum drawdown closes the account — you can restart an evaluation immediately, and existing subscribers get one free reset each month.

Can I use automated strategies?

Yes. Algorithmic and semi-automated systems are allowed, as are scalping, news trading and holding through economic releases. What's not allowed is exploiting simulated fills or copy-trading across many accounts to game the evaluation.

Which markets and platforms are supported?

CME equity index, energy, metals and FX futures, including all micro contracts. Accounts work with the major desktop and web futures platforms — you'll pick yours during onboarding.

How long until I get paid?

Payout requests are reviewed the same business day and settled within one to three days after that, depending on your withdrawal method.

Do most people pass?

No. Most traders who start an evaluation do not reach a funded account, and most funded accounts do not reach a large payout. Treat the fee as the cost of a test you may not pass.
